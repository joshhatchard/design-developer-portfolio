STORYTELLING & DESIGNING WITH COUNTRY

INTRODUCTION

Storytelling and Designing with Country focuses on embedding cultural knowledge, spirituality and connection into design. Page and Memmott (2024) highlight how objects and materials carry stories that link design practice to First Nations’ knowledge and Country.

DESIGNER

Alison Page is a Walbanga and Wadi Wadi designer and filmmaker who works through Designing with Country in treating land as a living partner in design. Her projects, like The Message: The Story from the Shore (2020), use storytelling to connect culture, people, and place. Page challenges Western design ideas by showing that true design begins with listening to and respecting Country.

CRITIQUE

Storytelling in design can make work meaningful and culturally rich, but it risks being superficial if not deeply grounded. It challenges designers to listen, collaborate, and prioritise authenticity over aesthetics, making design more relational and context-driven.
