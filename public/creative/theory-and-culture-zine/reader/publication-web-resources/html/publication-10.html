POLICY DESIGN

INTRODUCTION

Policy design focuses on applying design thinking to governance and public decision making. Mortati et al. (2022) highlight how design-led approaches can improve policy processes, foster innovation and create more responsive and human-centered governance systems.

DESIGNER

Jane MacMaster is a prominent Australian designer and engineer known for integrating design thinking into public policy. She played a pivotal role in establishing the Policy Design Discipline within the Australian Good Design Awards, aiming to elevate the role of design in policy-making. This initiative encourages the development of well-designed policies across various sectors including public, not-for-profit, and private organisations.

CRITIQUE

Policy design shapes societal systems by structuring decisions and regulations but can be overly rigid, slow to adapt and disconnected from lived experiences. Its focus on formal processes may restrain creativity and local innovation resulting in solutions that are technically sound but socially insensitive or difficult for communities to engage with effectively.
