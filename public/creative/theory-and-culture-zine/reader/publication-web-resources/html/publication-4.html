CONSUMERISM

INTRODUCTION

Consumerism examines how market forces and cultural values, shape design and user behaviour. Gaughwin (2023) highlights how products not only serve function but also influence identity, habits and social roles, reflecting broader economic and cultural dynamics.

DESIGNER

Barbara Kruger is a designer and artist who explores consumerism through bold graphic interventions. She critiques advertising, branding and mass media highlighting the persuasive power of design in shaping desires and identity. An example is “I shop therefore I am” (1987), which questions the role of consumer culture in defining self-worth.

CRITIQUE

Consumerism heavily influences design by driving trends and aesthetics, often prioritising profit over sustainability or meaningful user experience. This focus can lead to superficial and short-lived designs that encourage overconsumption, ultimately compromising the social and environmental responsibilities of design practice.
