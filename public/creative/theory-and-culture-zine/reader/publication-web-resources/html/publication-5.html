CRITICAL DESIGN

INTRODUCTION

Critical Design examines how design can question assumptions in provoking reflection and exploring social implications. Horst and Mohammid (2022) highlight how interactive and speculative approaches reveal the cultural, technological and ethical dimensions of products and systems encouraging designers and audiences to critically engage with their impact.

DESIGNER

Dunne & Raby are designers known for their work in critical design. They challenge assumptions and provoke reflection on technology, society, and the future. Through speculative projects like Technological Dreams Series they engage audiences to question everyday practices and explore the ethical, cultural and social implications of design decisions.

CRITIQUE

Critical design challenges conventional norms by provoking reflection and questioning societal values but it can sometimes be overly abstract or inaccessible which limits its practical impact. While intellectually stimulating, its focus on critique over usability may alienate broader audiences and make it less effective in driving tangible change or influencing mainstream design practice.
