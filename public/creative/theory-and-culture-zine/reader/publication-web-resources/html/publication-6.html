SERVICE DESIGN

INTRODUCTION

Service Design focuses on creating and improving systems of interactions, processes and experiences. Kimbell and Blomberg (2017) highlight its emphasis on the relational and dynamic aspects of services in showing how design shapes the overall experience beyond individual products.

DESIGNER

Paul Harvey is an Australian service designer who led an ecosystem mapping project for myGov, a key platform of Services Australia. His work involved creating 21 detailed customer journey maps and a comprehensive service design blueprint to enhance coordination and improve service delivery for Australian citizens.

CRITIQUE

Service design improves user experiences by coordinating touch points and optimising processes, yet it can become overly procedural by prioritising efficiency over creativity or human nuance. Its focus on systemisation may overlook emotional or cultural dimensions, risking experiences that are smooth but impersonal or functional but lacking in meaningful engagement.
