CIRCULAR DESIGN

INTRODUCTION

Circular Design and Ethics focus on creating sustainable and responsible design practices. Buwert (2017) emphasises the ethical foundations of design, highlighting how designers can consider long-term impacts, resource use and societal responsibility in shaping products, systems and environments.

DESIGNER

Ellen MacArthur is a designer and advocate for circular design, promoting systems that minimise waste and extend product life cycles. Through the Ellen MacArthur Foundation she encourages designers to create products and services that can be reused, refurbished or recycled to foster sustainable consumption and reduce environmental impact globally.

CRITIQUE

Circular design promotes sustainability by encouraging reuse, repair and resource efficiency but it can face practical and economic limitations. Its focus on environmental goals may compromise aesthetics, affordability or convenience. Without systemic support, circular products risk being niche rather than mainstream, limiting their broader impact on consumption and design culture.
