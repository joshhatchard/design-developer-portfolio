PLURIVERSAL DESIGN

INTRODUCTION

Pluriversal Design focuses on embracing diverse perspectives and knowledge systems in design. Noel et al. (2023) highlight how it promotes inclusive and context sensitive practices that recognise multiple ways of knowing. It enables design to address social, cultural and ecological complexities.

DESIGNER

Fernando Laposse, a pluriversal designer from Mexico who integrates indigenous knowledge and sustainable practices into design. He collaborates with local communities to restore ecosystems and cultural heritage. His project “Totomoxtle”uses colourful corn husks to create veneers, highlighting biodiversity loss while empowering indigenous farmers through traditional agriculture and craft.

CRITIQUE

Pluriversal design champions inclusivity by valuing diverse cultural perspectives and local knowledge, yet it can be challenging to implement in standardised systems or global markets. Its emphasis on context specific solutions may slow scalability and without careful mediation, it risks tokenism or superficial representation rather than truly empowering communities.
