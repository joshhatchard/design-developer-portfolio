POSTHUMAN DESIGN

INTRODUCTION

Post-human design focuses on collaborating with non-human agents and environments. Mehl et al. (2025) emphasise designing-with rather than for, highlighting relational and co-creative practices that integrate humans, technologies and ecological systems into design processes.

DESIGNER

Behnaz Farahi is an architect and designer whose work exemplifies posthuman design by integrating technology, into fashion and the human body. Her project “Returning the Gaze” (2022) features a wearable robotic installation that responds to the wearer‘s eye movements, challenging traditional notions of surveillance and the male gaze in fashion. This project exemplifies how posthuman design can explore the intersections of technology, identity and societal norms.

CRITIQUE

Posthuman design challenges human-centred assumptions by integrating nonhuman actors and technologies, but it can become conceptually abstract and ethically ambiguous. Its emphasis on decentering humans may overlook practical usability and social needs making designs intellectually provocative yet difficult to apply meaningfully in everyday contexts.
